About us

Built in Kenya, for Kenyan campaigns.

Kenyan elections are won ward by ward — in the matatu stage, the market, the church compound and the WhatsApp group. Most campaign software was built for somewhere else. We built ours around Kenya’s seats, counties and wards, Kiswahili and English, M-Pesa and SMS, and the realities of election day.

Lawful and accountable

Consent before contact, audit trails on every change and export, and tools for data-subject requests under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

Evidence over noise

Analysis cites its sources. Where data is missing the platform says so — it never invents results or polls.

For the whole team

Candidates, managers, finance, field officers and agents each see what they need — on the phone in their pocket.

Ready to run a smarter campaign?

From one ward to the whole republic: field teams, voter data, messaging, intelligence and election day in one secure platform.
